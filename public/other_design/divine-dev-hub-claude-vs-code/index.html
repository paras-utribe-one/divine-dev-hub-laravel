Frequently asked

Straight answers to the questions we get every week.

Still have a question?

Ask us directly
How much does custom software cost?
Most projects range from $15,000 for a focused MVP to $150,000+ for a full enterprise platform. We provide a fixed estimate after a free scoping call — or try the cost calculator for an instant range.
How long does a typical project take?
Most projects run 8–20 weeks. An MVP typically ships in 10–12 weeks; larger platforms are scoped in phases so you see working software every two weeks.
Who owns the code and IP?
You do — 100%, on delivery. Full source code and IP transfer, no exceptions, no retained licensing.
Do you sign NDAs?
Always, before we discuss specifics. We'll sign yours or send ours — whichever you prefer.
How do you handle scope changes?
Change requests are quoted upfront with a clear cost and timeline impact before any work starts — no surprise invoices.
What if I already have a codebase?
We regularly take over existing projects. A short technical audit up front tells us — and you — exactly what state it's in before we commit to a timeline.
How do you protect our data?
GDPR-aligned processes, access-controlled environments, and NDAs on every engagement. See our certifications & compliance page for the full picture.
What happens after launch?
Every project includes a support window, and ongoing SLA-backed maintenance plans are available for as long as you need them.
